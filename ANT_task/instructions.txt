[welcome]
Help the birds get back home!

Look at the bird in the MIDDLE of the row.
If the middle bird is YELLOW, press G.
If the middle bird is RED, press H.
Ignore the other birds.

Let's practise first.
Press SPACE to begin. ESC = stop.

[practice_reminder]
Let's try again! Help the middle bird get home.

YELLOW middle bird: press G.
RED middle bird: press H.
Ignore the other birds.

Press SPACE to continue. ESC = stop.

[test_start]
Now let's start the game!

Look at the MIDDLE bird, not its neighbours.
YELLOW: press G. RED: press H.
Answer as quickly and accurately as you can.

Press SPACE to start. ESC = stop.

[block_break]
Time for a short break!

Press SPACE when you are ready to continue.
ESC = stop.

[pause]
Study paused.

Press SPACE to continue.
Press ESC again to stop.

[stopped]
Study stopped. Your data have been saved.

Press SPACE to close.

[end]
Experiment complete. Thank you!

Press SPACE to close.
